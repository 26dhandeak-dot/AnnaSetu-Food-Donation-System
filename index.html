How AnnaSetu works

Three simple steps can make a meaningful difference.

1. Share food

Donors list safe surplus food with quantity, location and pickup time.

2. Connect

Nearby NGOs discover available donations and request a pickup.

Food reaches communities and every completed donation counts.
